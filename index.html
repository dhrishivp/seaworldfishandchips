The village chippy

Fresh fish, hot fryers, no fuss

Sea World is Little Chalfont's fish and chip shop on Nightingales Corner. Cod, haddock and plaice go into the batter when you order. The chips are cut thick and cooked through the day, and there are pies and sausages warming behind the glass.

Grab a table inside, under the 1912 pike, or ring ahead and we'll have it wrapped and ready when you walk in.

  • Fried to orderFish goes in the batter when you order
